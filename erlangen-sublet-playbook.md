# Subletting a room in Erlangen, 1 Oct – 31 Dec 2026

Research report and action playbook. Compiled 22 September 2026.

---

## 0. Scope note

This report does **not** contain a harvested list of students' emails and phone
numbers. Scraping personal contact data from housing sites and cold-contacting
people with it is unlawful under the GDPR in Germany (no legal basis for
unsolicited processing), breaches the terms of every major portal, and gets
accounts banned — which would cost you the listings you actually need.

What follows instead is the mechanism that works: put the room where seekers
already look, and reply to people who have **publicly posted that they are
looking** through the channel they chose to be reached on. On several Erlangen
boards, seekers publish their own email addresses specifically so that landlords
will write to them. That is consent, and it converts far better than a cold mail.

---

## 1. The single most important finding: reframe the offer

FAU's winter semester 2026/27 lecture period runs **Monday 12 October 2026 to
mid-February 2027**; the semester itself runs 1 October – 31 March.

Your window ends 31 December — roughly six weeks *before* lectures end. A normal
incoming student needs a room until February or July, so if you advertise
"student room, October to December" you will be filtered out by almost everyone
searching.

**Advertise it as a `Zwischenmiete` (interim sublet) instead.** The three-month
furnished bridge room is its own market segment in Erlangen, and in October it is
a seller's market. Rank your target audiences in this order:

| Rank | Audience | Why the 3-month window fits |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Newly arrived students with no room yet** | Erlangen's October crunch is severe; Studierendenwerk halls have waiting lists. Hundreds arrive in October with nothing and will take a room to New Year gratefully while they keep hunting for something permanent. This is your biggest pool. |
| 2 | **Interns / Werkstudenten** at Siemens Healthineers, Siemens Energy, Uniklinikum, and the Erlangen tech cluster | Internships are commonly 3–6 months and many cohorts start in October. They need furnished, short, and hassle-free — exactly your product. |
| 3 | **Medical students** at Uniklinikum Erlangen | Famulatur blocks (~30 days) and PJ tertials (16 weeks) start on fixed dates including autumn; they rotate through the city constantly. |
| 4 | **Guest researchers / visiting PhDs** at FAU and Max Planck | Typically 1–6 month stays, often institutionally funded, usually the least price-sensitive and least likely to haggle. |
| 5 | **Exchange students on a short-track programme** | Some exchange schemes run only to Christmas; ISN-NEF sees these arrivals directly. |
| 6 | **People between flats** — new hires waiting on a permanent lease, people whose own move-in slipped | Year-round demand, and they will decide fast. |

A secondary angle worth one line in the ad: *"Anschlussmieter/in ab Januar
möglich, wenn du bleiben möchtest — sprich mich an"* if there is any chance the
room could continue. It converts audience 1 from a compromise into a real option.
If it genuinely cannot continue, say so plainly and early instead — see §7.

## 3.5 The demand side: where people post that they are looking

You are the one giving out the room, so the highest-value work is not polishing
your own ad — it is reading the ads of people who have already written "I am
looking". Those people are pre-qualified, they state their dates, and almost no
landlord bothers to answer them. Below is the complete seeker map for Erlangen
and its surroundings.

### Set alerts, then answer within the hour

In the last week of September, a "suche Zimmer" post in Erlangen collects
replies within minutes. Turn on **instant email alerts** for saved searches on

them, because they are stressed and they have nowhere to sleep.

### Who "around Erlangen" actually is

Two separate flows, and the first is the one people miss.

**1. People who work around Erlangen but need to live in Erlangen.** This is
your best-fit, least-contested audience.

- **Herzogenaurach — Adidas, Puma, Schaeffler.** Three global headquarters in a
  town of ~24,000 with a chronically thin housing market. Their intern and
  working-student cohorts arrive in waves, stay three to six months, and many
  end up living in Erlangen and commuting the ~20 minutes by bus, because
  Herzogenaurach itself has nothing. Other landlords already know this — there
  are live WG-Gesucht ads headlined *"ideal für Adidas und Puma Praktikanten"*.
  Put that exact phrase in your own ad and search Herzogenaurach as a seeker
  location too.
- **Fraunhofer IIS** (the largest Fraunhofer institute, in Erlangen) and the
  **Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light** (Staudtstraße 2, Erlangen)
  both run international research schools and take in guest researchers,
  interns and thesis students on short contracts year-round. They are
  institutionally funded, punctual, and leave on the agreed date.
- **Siemens Healthineers / Siemens Energy** interns and Werkstudenten in
  Erlangen itself.
- **Uniklinikum Erlangen** — Famulatur and PJ cohorts rotating on fixed dates.

**2. People searching the wider area who would happily take Erlangen.** Anyone
already looking in the commuter belt is a candidate, because Erlangen is the
centre of gravity, not the compromise:

- On the S1 line (Nürnberg – Erlangen – Forchheim – Bamberg): **Bubenreuth,
  Baiersdorf, Forchheim, Fürth, Nürnberg**.
- On the Erlangen bus network: **Uttenreuth and Buckenhof** (lines 209/210,
  direct to the Humanities Centre), **Spardorf and Bubenreuth** (line 253),
  **Effeltrich, Poxdorf, Langensendelbach, Marloffstein, Möhrendorf** (line
  208 and neighbours).
- **Nürnberg and Fürth** hold FAU's WiSo faculty, TH Nürnberg Ohm and UTN.
  Weaker fit — those students want to be near their own campus — but worth one
  pass through the seeker lists above, especially for anyone whose ad mentions
  an Erlangen internship or clinic placement.

Mention the transport facts explicitly in your ad: the S-Bahn line, the bus
number, and that the VGN Semesterticket covers the area. For someone deciding
from abroad, "line 253, twelve minutes" is worth more than "gut angebunden".

### A different kind of outreach: go through the gatekeepers

Rather than contacting individuals one by one, one email to the right
coordinator reaches an entire arriving cohort, and they will forward it because
they field housing panic every October:

- HR / early-talent or intern coordination at **Adidas, Puma and Schaeffler**
  (Herzogenaurach) and **Siemens Healthineers** (Erlangen) — ask them to pass a
  room offer to interns starting in October.
- The **international office or IMPRS coordinator** at Fraunhofer IIS and MPL.
- **ISN-NEF**, the Erasmus-network student association for Erlangen–Nürnberg–Fürth.
- The **FAU Welcome Center** (Helmstraße 1, entrance B, Wed/Thu 9–12) and
  `uni-gaestehaus@fau.de`, who turn people away when the guest houses are full.
- **Praktikums- or Studiengangskoordination** at two or three large FAU
  departments, and the Uniklinikum PJ/Famulatur office.

Keep these emails to five lines: what the room is, the exact dates, the price,
that Anmeldung is possible, and one line asking them to forward it.

## 4. Pricing

Erlangen market reference, 2026:

- City-wide average asking rent: **≈ €14.21/m²**
- One-room flats: **≈ €17.46/m²** (small units carry a big premium)
- Range by location: **≈ €11.61 – €17.63/m²**
- Up ~3.6% year on year
- FAU quotes private-market single rooms from ~€150 and shared flats from ~€175
  at the very bottom of the market; realistic furnished WG rooms sit well above
  that
- Erlangen's official Mietspiegel (open data): <https://erlangen.de/themenseite/informationsservice/open-data/mietspiegel>

How to set your number:

1. **Anchor on what you pay.** For a sublet of your own room the fair and
   legally safest figure is your own rent for that room plus a modest,
   justifiable share of utilities — and a furnishing surcharge if you are
   leaving your furniture. Charging conspicuously more than you pay invites
   trouble with your landlord and, in extreme cases, is a criminal offence
   (Mietwucher) at roughly 20%+ above the local comparative rent.
2. **Quote one all-inclusive `Warmmiete`.** Short-term tenants will not do a
   Nebenkosten reconciliation over three months. One number including heating,
   electricity, internet and, if you have it, the Rundfunkbeitrag is a strong
   selling point — say `Warmmiete, alle Nebenkosten und WLAN inklusive`.
3. **A furnished 3-month stay legitimately commands a premium** of roughly
   20–40% over an unfurnished annual let, because the tenant buys nothing and
   moves in with a suitcase. On HousingAnywhere and Wunderflats, price toward
   the top of your range; on WG-Gesucht and the student boards, price for speed.
4. **Sanity-check live, today.** Open the Erlangen WG-Gesucht list and the
   "Auf Zeit" Kleinanzeigen category, filter to furnished rooms available from
   1 October, and place yourself just below the median for comparable size and
   location. In late September the market is tight enough that a fairly priced
   room lets in days.
5. **Deposit:** ask one month, maximum two. Legally a residential deposit is
   capped at three months' basic rent, but for a three-month stay anything above
   one month reads as a red flag to good tenants.

## 5. What makes a Zwischenmiete ad convert

Non-negotiables, in order of how much they matter for a short let:

1. **Exact dates in the headline.** `Zwischenmiete 01.10. – 31.12.2026` removes
   all ambiguity and is what people filter on.
2. **8–12 photos, daylight, tidied room.** Include the kitchen, the bathroom,
   the view from the window and the building entrance. Ads without a bathroom
   photo get skipped. This single factor outweighs price.
3. **"Fully furnished, move in with a suitcase."** Bed, mattress, bedding,
   desk, chair, wardrobe, lamp, kitchenware — list what stays.
4. **One all-in price.** See §4.
5. **Commute facts, not adjectives.** Minutes on foot or by bike to the
   relevant FAU campus (Südgelände / Innenstadt), to Uniklinikum, and to the
   nearest bus stop or S-Bahn; whether the Semesterticket covers it.
6. **Anmeldung confirmed.** "Anmeldung möglich, Wohnungsgeberbestätigung wird
   gestellt" is a decisive selling point for internationals — many short lets
   refuse it, and without it a tenant cannot register, which blocks their bank
   account, visa and enrolment. See §7.
7. **Both languages in one ad.** German first, then a full English version. Half
   your best-paying audience does not read German.
8. **Who else lives there**, if it is a WG: number of flatmates, ages, what
   they study or do, how loud the flat is.
9. **Say what you want back**: "kurze Vorstellung, Studien- oder
   Praktikumsnachweis, Video-Call zur Besichtigung möglich".

## 7. Legal and admin checklist — do not skip item 1

**1. Get your landlord's written permission first.** Under §540 BGB you may not
hand the flat to a third party without the landlord's consent. §553(1) BGB gives
you a *right* to permission to sublet **part** of the home when a legitimate
interest arises after the lease began — a semester abroad, an internship
elsewhere, or needing to cover the rent are all textbook legitimate interests.
Note the limits that matter for you:

- The right under §553 covers **part** of the home, not handing over the whole
  flat. If your room is one room in a shared flat, you are squarely within it.
  If you are subletting an entire single-occupancy flat, your claim is weaker
  and you need actual consent.
- Subletting **without** permission is a breach of contract and can justify
  termination of your own lease. This is the one risk in the whole project that
  can genuinely hurt you.
- **In a Studierendenwerk hall of residence, subletting is usually restricted or
  forbidden outright.** If your room is in a hall, ask the administration in
  writing before you advertise anything — the answer may be no.
- Ask by email so you have it in writing, name the subtenant, the exact dates and
  the rent, and keep the reply.
- §553 BGB: <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__553.html>
- Plain-language guide with a model contract: <https://www.finanztip.de/untermiete/>

**2. Use a written Untermietvertrag (sublease contract).** Fixed term 01.10. –
31.12.2026, stating: rent and what it includes, deposit and how it is returned,
handover inventory of furniture with photos, meter readings if applicable, house
rules, and that the main lease governs. Free German templates are widely
available; Finanztip above includes one. A fixed end date is what protects you
from a tenant who will not leave — do not let this be a handshake.

**3. Issue the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung.** As the main tenant subletting, **you
become the `Wohnungsgeber`** and are legally obliged to issue this confirmation
so your subtenant can register at the Einwohnermeldeamt. It must be in their
hands in time to register within two weeks of moving in. Failing to issue it, or
issuing it late or wrongly, is an administrative offence fined up to €1,000.

- Erlangen's form and information: <https://erlangen.de/service/32146>
- Advertise this as a feature. It is one of the main reasons short lets fall
  through for international tenants.

**4. Keep it a genuine residential let, not short-stay guest accommodation.**
Erlangen's Zweckentfremdungsverbotssatzung has applied city-wide since
7 February 2020. Using a dwelling for **guest accommodation for more than eight
weeks in a calendar year** — the Airbnb pattern — requires a permit. One
subtenant living there for three months as their registered residence is normal
residential use and is not affected. Do not split the window into a string of
short holiday-style bookings: that is what crosses the line.

- <https://erlangen.de/service/24654>

**5. Practicalities to settle before the tenant moves in**

- **What happens on 1 January?** Decide now and say it in the ad. If you are
  returning, be explicit that the end date is hard. If the room could continue,
  say an Anschlussmiete is negotiable — it doubles your applicant pool.
- **Deposit handling:** one month, transferred by bank, receipted in the
  contract. Never cash.
- **Handover protocol** with photos and meter readings, signed by both, on
  move-in and move-out.
- **Insurance:** tell your Haftpflicht/Hausrat insurer about the sublet; some
  policies restrict cover for subtenants' belongings, and your own contents are
  likely not covered against a subtenant.
- **Rundfunkbeitrag (€18.36/month per household):** clarify in writing who pays
  it, and that the subtenant should not register a second time for the same flat.
- **Flatmates:** if it is a WG, get your flatmates' agreement before you list
  anything, and let them meet the candidate. A sublet that the flatshare resents
  goes wrong fast.
- **Tax:** sublet income is taxable in principle; if you charge no more than your
  own costs there is typically nothing to declare, but keep the numbers.

## 8. Screening and scam avoidance

October is peak season for rental fraud in German university towns, in both
directions. Working rules:

- **Keep all first contact on the platform.** Move to email or WhatsApp only
  after a real conversation.
- **Never accept an overpayment**, a cheque, a "shipping agent", a payment from
  a third party, or anyone who wants to send money before viewing. The
  overpayment-and-refund scam targets landlords specifically.
- **Do a video call** with anyone who cannot view in person, and have them show
  the room list they are working from. Five minutes of conversation filters
  almost everything.
- **Ask for proof of purpose** — enrolment certificate, internship contract or
  employer letter — plus ID at signing, and one reference if the stay is short.
- **Deposit and rent by SEPA bank transfer only**, to your own account, never by
  PayPal friends-and-family, gift card, Western Union or crypto.
- **Refuse pressure.** Anyone who wants to sign and pay without seeing the room
  or reading the contract is either a scammer or a future problem.
- **Do not put your phone number or address in the public ad text.** Let the
  platform relay first contact; share details with shortlisted candidates only.

## 9. Seven-day action plan

You have nine days until 1 October and lectures start 12 October. Demand peaks
now and falls off a cliff in November, so front-load everything.

**Day 1 (today)**
- Email your landlord for written subletting permission. Nothing else matters
  until this is in hand. If you live in a hall, ask the administration.
- Take photos: room, kitchen, bathroom, building entrance, view. Daylight, tidy.
- Measure the room, count the minutes to campus, settle your all-in price.

**Day 2**
- Publish on WG-Gesucht (limited term, exact dates) and Kleinanzeigen.
- Register the room with the Studierendenwerk Privatzimmervermittlung.
- Post to the Hochschul-SMD list and the ESG/KHG board.

**Day 3**
- Work the seeker side: WG-Gesucht Gesuche, Kleinanzeigen Wohnungs-Gesuche, the
  SMD requests. Send individual, specific messages — 15 to 25 of them.
- Set up instant alerts for new seeker ads in both portals.

**Day 4**
- Add Tier 2 portals: studenten-wohnung.de, wg-liste.de, wg-suche.de,
  studenten-wg.de, ImmoScout24, immowelt, meinestadt.
- List on HousingAnywhere and Wunderflats at the higher price point.

**Day 5**
- Email ISN-NEF, the FAU Welcome Center, `uni-gaestehaus@fau.de`, Stuve Referat
  Soziales, HC24, and two or three department or Uniklinikum coordination
  offices.
- Post in the Facebook and Telegram/WhatsApp groups.

**Day 6–7**
- Run viewings, in person and by video call. Expect a lot of traffic.
- Prepare the Untermietvertrag and the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung form in advance
  so you can sign the moment you pick someone.
- If enquiries are thin by day 5, the cause is almost always photos or price, in
  that order — fix the photos first, then cut the price by 5–10%.
