[**Why you, specifically.** One or two sentences naming something this organisation

I am a software developer in Bremen. I have built a free, ad-free tool that helps
households work out which German care benefits they are entitled to, and how much is
going unclaimed:

**<https://solution-for-germany.vercel.app>**

Care benefits in Germany are not paid automatically. They have to be applied for,
separately, in language most families do not speak, and money already awarded routinely
goes uncollected. The people who lose most are the ones least equipped to fight it.

Three things make it different from the tools in this space. It runs entirely in the
browser — no account, no server, no analytics, and tapped answers never leave the device.
Every figure carries its statutory source and the date a human last checked it; what
cannot be traced is not shown. And it stops asking once the answer can no longer change,
turning sixty-four questions into twelve–twenty, with the reduction proved rather than
estimated.

It is honest about what it is not: an estimate, not an assessment, and it points people
at the free statutory advice they are entitled to.

[**What I am asking for:** one specific, small, cheap-to-grant thing. Thirty minutes. One
reviewer. One introduction. Never "a partnership".]
