I am a software developer based in Bremen. I have built a free, ad-free tool that helps
households work out which German care benefits (*Pflegeleistungen*) they are entitled to,
and how much of that money is going unclaimed:

**<https://solution-for-germany.vercel.app>**

**The problem.** Care benefits in Germany are not paid automatically. They have to be
applied for, separately, in language most families do not speak. Money that has already
been awarded routinely goes uncollected. The people who lose most are the ones least
equipped to fight it: the very old, the very tired, and families doing the paperwork in a
second language.

**What it does.** It reproduces the arithmetic of the *Neues Begutachtungsassessment*,
estimates the care grade an assessment would likely arrive at, compares the resulting
entitlement against what the household already receives, and lists what to do about the
difference, in order, with the statutory basis for each step. It ends in a PDF the family
can carry to the care insurer — carrying the estimate, the money, the next steps, and the
answers they gave, because the official assessment is an interview asking the same things.

**Three things that make it different from the tools in this space:**

1. **Nothing leaves the device.** It runs entirely in the browser. No account, no server,
   no analytics. I cannot see a single answer anyone gives it.
2. **Every figure is traceable.** Each amount and threshold carries its statutory source
   and the date a human last verified it. A figure that cannot be traced is not shown. The
   headline is deliberately conservative — a number that is too high is worse than no
   number, because the family finds out at the counter and never trusts the tool again.
3. **It stops asking when the answer can no longer change.** Twelve to twenty questions
   instead of the instrument's sixty-four, and the reduction is proved rather than
   estimated: bounds are computed on the unasked questions, and the intake ends only when
   those bounds agree. A partial intake yields a range, never a confident wrong grade.

It is honest about what it is not. It says plainly that it is an estimate rather than an
assessment, and it points users at the free statutory care advice they are legally
entitled to.
