# Contact Directory

Researched September 2026. Every address below was found on the organisation's own
site or an official register; sources are linked per entry. **Re-check the address and
any named person on the organisation's own page immediately before sending** — staff
change, and a bounced first contact is a wasted shot.

⚠️ not verified, use the site's contact form

## Tier E — Funding routes

Ordered by fit, not by size of cheque.

### F1. Prototype Fund — *best fit by a distance*

Public-interest open-source software by independent developers and small teams. Anspruch
is almost a textbook fit: open source, public interest, solo developer, no business
model required.

- 📧 `info@prototypefund.de`
- Applications: <https://bewerben.prototypefund.de/> · <https://www.prototypefund.de/en/application>
- **Round:** applications for the next cohort open **1 October – 30 November 2026**.
  That is the nearest hard deadline in this document — plan backwards from it.
- **Blocker:** the repo has no licence. Fix before applying.
- **Draft:** `emails/F1-prototype-fund.md`

### F2. GKV-Spitzenverband — Modellprogramm § 8 Abs. 3 / 3a SGB XI

Funds independent projects developing the *Pflegeversicherung* itself. Two-stage:
*Projektskizze* first. Up to five years. Scientific standards expected — realistically
needs a university partner (Constructor University or Universität Bremen).

- <https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/pflegeversicherung/forschung/modellprojekte/modellprojekte.jsp>
- Leitfaden für Projektskizzen (§ 8 Abs. 3 SGB XI):
  <https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/media/dokumente/pflegeversicherung/forschung/antragstellung/20240927_Leitfaden_Projektskizzen__8_Abs._3_SGB_XI.pdf>
- ⚠️ Submission address is in the Leitfaden PDF — read it before writing.
- **Realistic timeline:** 12+ months. Start the academic partnership conversation now.

## Deliberately not on this list

- **Private care-tech companies and Pflegegrad "Antragshilfe" services.** They are
  competitors with a lead-generation business model. Approaching them early gives away
  the method and buys nothing.
- **Pflegekassen other than AOK Bremen, at this stage.** Approach one, learn from the
  reply, then decide whether the others are worth it.
